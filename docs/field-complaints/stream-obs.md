# Field complaints: stream, OBS and PC setup

Date: 2026-11-05. Scope: stream/OBS/PC only (mic and camera hardware are covered elsewhere).

## Method and limits
- Web search and page fetch. Reddit threads, Twitch help pages and the OBS KB "encoding overloaded" page were mostly NOT reachable directly (404 or only search snippets). Where a row relies on search-result summaries of OBS forum threads or on third-party guides, it is marked (forum) or (guide). Sources are paraphrased.
- "Accepted fix" means the fix named by OBS staff/KB or repeated across several threads; forum threads often do not show an explicit "solved" mark. Anything I could not source is marked **unverified**.
- Frequency ranking is my judgement from how many threads/guides exist per topic, not a measured count (unverified as a statistic).
- "In Setup Coach today?" was checked by grep of `core/src/system/ids.rs`, `core/src/health/mod.rs` (ids at ~line 383), `core/src/system/encoder.rs`, `core/src/system/obs_audio.rs`.

## Ranked: 10 biggest gaps to build next
Ranked by (how often the complaint appears in sources) x (how detectable and fixable it is without AI).

1. **Capture black-screen diagnosis (rows 4, 5).** Highest-volume "OBS does not show my game" class; detect OBS elevation vs foreground game, overlay conflicts (RTSS/Afterburner), exclusive fullscreen, per-app GPU preference, and a GetSourceScreenshot black-frame test. Guided fix: run elevated, borderless, same GPU.
2. **GPU headroom / rendering-lag cause finder (row 3, 2).** The app already sees rendering/encoding lag; add the "why": GPU utilisation near 100%, uncapped game fps, OBS elevation (GPU priority), Multi-adapter flag, Game DVR. Offer cap-fps guidance and apply the reversible parts.
3. **Windows performance toggles (row 6).** Read HAGS, Game Mode, Game DVR and power plan; recommend and re-measure one change at a time (sources conflict, so measure rather than assume).
4. **Desktop/game audio path (rows 15, 16, 13).** Check OBS desktop device vs Windows default render device, Discord routed elsewhere, per-app audio session devices, and a stream-mix balance check from meter peaks (voice vs game vs music, master peak).
5. **Scene collection and profile backup/restore (row 20).** Automatic versioned backups of `%AppData%\obs-studio` and one-click restore, with detection of a suddenly empty collection or missing `.json`, and `.bak` recovery guidance. Cheap, high trust value.
6. **Bufferbloat / loaded-latency test (row 11).** Measure RTT idle vs under an upload load, explain in plain language, and give the router SQM/cap advice; also feed the bitrate headroom recommendation.
7. **OBS log parser (rows 21, 22, 2).** Read the latest OBS log for plugin load failures, encoder init failures, crashes and "wrong architecture" VST messages; turn them into findings.
8. **Recording to disk health (row 19, 18).** Check free space and write speed of the recording path, warn about same drive as game, and verify auto-remux and MKV/Hybrid; extends the existing recording_plain_mp4 finding.
9. **Encoder detail beyond choice (row 9).** Preset, lookahead, psycho-visual, B-frames, and AV1 eligibility from GPU model and platform; consolidate into one "encoder profile for your GPU" finding.
10. **A/V sync check and scene-weight audit (rows 12, 23).** Detect duplicate embedded-plus-separate audio on capture devices and test sync drift with a short recording; flag heavy browser sources and oversized filters.
